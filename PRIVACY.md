# Radio — Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Radio 2.3.1

## The short version
Radio collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reports, it doesn't use Google services, and it has no server of its own. What you save stays on your phone. To play radio and podcasts, the app connects directly to the services listed below, which see your IP address like any website.

## Who is responsible
Tintako, Croatia · app@tintako.com

## What the app stores, and where
Only on your phone, in the app's private storage:
- your favorites and your own stream links;
- the podcasts you follow, their episodes, notes and listening progress;
- the language you chose, the sleep timer and audio output settings;
- saved station lists, so they open without internet;
- which station you heard last, so that a play button outside the app (for example in a car) can start Radio again.

The app is left out of phone backups. An OPML file with your podcast list is saved only where you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

## Permissions, and what each is for
- **Internet:** radio, podcasts and the station directory.
- **Network state:** to tell "no internet" from "station directory not responding".
- **Foreground service, media playback and wake lock:** to keep playing with the screen off.
- **Notifications:** playback controls (Android 13 and newer ask once).
- **Camera:** only while you scan a QR code with a link, asked the first time; images are never saved or sent.

## What leaves, and only when you ask
Something leaves the phone only when you use a feature that needs it: browsing or searching stations, playing a station or an episode, adding or refreshing a podcast, or sending audio to a speaker. The app doesn't retry by itself in the background.

## Who else receives anything
- **Radio Browser** (radio-browser.info): your station search (name, country, language, genre). When you play a station from the directory, its ID is sent so the directory can count the play.
- **The station's server:** the audio stream, while the station plays.
- **The server of the station's logo:** the logo, when the player shows it.
- **Podcast servers:** the feed when you add or refresh a podcast, and the episode audio when you listen.
- **Speakers on your Wi-Fi (Sonos, DLNA):** only while Audio output is open and while a speaker plays; the speaker gets the address and title of the station or episode, and fetches the audio itself.

Each of them sees your IP address, like any website. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing passes through Tintako.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)
Tintako doesn't process your personal data. The connections above happen at your request, to provide the feature you use; each service handles your IP address under its own terms.

## Your rights
Because Tintako doesn't process your data, there is nothing to hand over or delete; your data stays on your phone and you can delete it by uninstalling the app. You can complain to your national data protection authority (in Croatia, AZOP).

## Children
Radio is suitable for a general audience and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children. Stations marked as adult content are not listed.

## Changes
This policy may change with a new app version. The version in the app (Settings → About → Privacy) and this page are kept the same.
